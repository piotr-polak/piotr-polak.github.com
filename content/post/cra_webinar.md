*This webinar took place on February 3, 2026.*

On February 3, 2026, I presented an overview of the [Cyber Resilience Act](<https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng>) with The Lighting Industry Association. The session covered the key product-security and process requirements, and what manufacturers need to do to achieve and maintain compliance.

{{< figure src="/images/cra_webinar.jpg" alt="Announcement for the CRA webinar held with The Lighting Industry Association on February 3, 2026" caption="Original announcement for the webinar held on February 3, 2026." >}}

The webinar was open to The Lighting Industry Association members. Registration is closed.

For current information, start with the [Cyber Resilience Act guide]({{< relref "cra" >}}) and the [reporting obligations article]({{< relref "post/cra_reporting_obligations" >}}).
