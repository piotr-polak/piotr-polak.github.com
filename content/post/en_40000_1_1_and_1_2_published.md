CEN and CENELEC have [announced](<https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2026/en-in-the-spotlight/2026-11-19-cra-standards-en-40000-1-1-and-en-40000-1-2/>) the publication of the first two European Standards in the EN 40000 series supporting the [Cyber Resilience Act]({{< relref "cra" >}}):

-   [EN 40000-1-1](<https://standards.cencenelec.eu/ords/f?p=CEN:110:::::FSP_PROJECT,FSP_ORG_ID:82349,2307986&cs=1233A32A252D91A47BFF714F1B8C1ED26>) "Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements - Part 1-1: Vocabulary"
-   [EN 40000-1-2](<https://standards.cencenelec.eu/ords/f?p=CEN:110:::::FSP_PROJECT,FSP_ORG_ID:81335,2307986&cs=1D72BA048927BF4E6076BA309587EA01A>) "Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements - Part 1-2: Principles, product risk management, and lifecycle activities"

Both were developed in CEN-CLC/JTC 13/WG 9, the working group for the horizontal CRA standards, in which I am contributing.
CEN-CENELEC reports more than 450 committee members from 28 national standardization organizations and 17 liaison organizations.
The standards are available through the national standards bodies, subject to their national publication schedules.


## What the two standards do {#what-they-do}

**EN 40000-1-1** sets the common terms and definitions for the whole EN 40000 series.
It may look like a small starting point, but the series brings together experts from many sectors who use the same words differently, and consistent terminology is what makes the more technical standards read the same way for everyone.

**EN 40000-1-2** is the process foundation.
It sets out general cybersecurity principles and a common approach to identifying, assessing and managing cybersecurity risks across the whole product lifecycle, from early design decisions through development, production and maintenance to the end of the support period and decommissioning.
The other horizontal standards, on vulnerability handling and generic security requirements, and the product-specific standards build on it.


## What this means for manufacturers {#manufacturers}

The CRA requires a documented cybersecurity risk assessment that is kept up to date during the support period.
EN 40000-1-2 is now the published reference for how to do that, and the natural place to start aligning product security processes with the CRA.

As noted when it was [approved]({{< relref "post/en_40000_1_2_approved" >}}), no citation in the Official Journal is expected for EN 40000-1-2 under the CRA, so it does not give presumption of conformity on its own.
Its value is the common method and vocabulary that the cited standards will rely on.


## What comes next {#next}

CEN-CENELEC expects FprEN 40000-1-3 on vulnerability handling and the first product-specific (vertical) standards to follow soon.
