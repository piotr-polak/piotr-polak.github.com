The European Commission adopted [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/339](<https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/339/oj/eng>) on **February 16, 2026**. Published in the Official Journal on April 29, 2026, it repeals the RED cybersecurity Delegated Act—Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30—with effect from **December 11, 2027**, when the main CRA requirements apply.


## What this means for manufacturers

Until December 11, 2027, radio equipment in scope must still meet the applicable RED DA cybersecurity requirements. The EN 18031 series provides a route to presumption of conformity, subject to the [restrictions in its Official Journal citations](<https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2025/138/oj/eng>).

The repeal does not remove the other requirements of the Radio Equipment Directive. It also does not end market surveillance of compliance with the RED DA requirements for equipment placed on the market between **August 1, 2025, and December 10, 2027**, as recital 5 of the repeal regulation makes clear.

Manufacturers should plan the transition of their assessments and documentation for products placed on the market from December 11, 2027. Under Article 69 of the CRA, products placed on the market before that date generally become subject to its requirements only if substantially modified from that date; the Article 14 reporting obligations already apply to earlier products in scope.
