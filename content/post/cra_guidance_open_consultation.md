The European Commission opened a public consultation on [draft guidance on the Cyber Resilience Act](<https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/16959-Draft-Commission-guidance-on-the-Cyber-Resilience-Act_en>) on **3 March 2026**. The feedback period ended on **13 April 2026** and is now closed.

The draft addresses several interpretation questions, including:

-   Free and open-source software
-   Substantial modifications and spare parts
-   Support period
-   Important and critical products
-   Cybersecurity risk assessment and integration of products and components
-   Remote data processing


## What this means for manufacturers

The draft explains the Commission's proposed interpretation of these issues; it should not be treated as final guidance or as a replacement for the regulation.
The consultation is no longer accepting feedback. Check the [Commission's initiative page](<https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/16959-Draft-Commission-guidance-on-the-Cyber-Resilience-Act_en>) for the documents, submitted feedback, and updates on adoption.

For background, see [Cyber Resilience Act: an Overview]({{< relref "post/cra_overview" >}}).
