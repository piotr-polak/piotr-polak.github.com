The European Commission opened a public consultation on [draft guidance on the Cyber Resilience Act](<https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/16959-Draft-Commission-guidance-on-the-Cyber-Resilience-Act_en>) on **3 March 2026**. The feedback period ended on **13 April 2026** and is now closed.

*Update, 11 November 2026: the Commission [published the guidance](<https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-new-guidance-support-timely-cyber-resilience-act-implementation>) on 27 July 2026 as C(2026) 5252. The Commission has approved its content and will formally adopt it once all language versions are available; the guidance applies from that date.*

The draft addresses several interpretation questions, including:

-   Free and open-source software
-   Substantial modifications and spare parts
-   Support period
-   Important and critical products
-   Cybersecurity risk assessment and integration of products and components
-   Remote data processing


## What this means for manufacturers

Use the [published guidance](<https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-new-guidance-support-timely-cyber-resilience-act-implementation>) rather than the consultation draft: it sets out the Commission's interpretation of scope (including remote data processing and free and open-source software), substantial modifications, support periods, reporting obligations, and risk assessment. It is not binding and does not replace the regulation.
The [Commission's initiative page](<https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/16959-Draft-Commission-guidance-on-the-Cyber-Resilience-Act_en>) still holds the consultation draft and the submitted feedback.
