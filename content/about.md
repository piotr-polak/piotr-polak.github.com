I am a cybersecurity expert who drives the development and certification of secure connected products.
Over the past twenty years at [Philips](<https://www.philips.com/>), [NXP](<https://www.nxp.com/>), and currently [Signify](<https://www.signify.com/>), I have worked in connected product development with a consistent focus on product security.
I contribute to global product security standards through
[CEN-CENELEC](<https://www.cencenelec.eu/>),
the [Connectivity Standards Alliance](<https://csa-iot.org/>), and
the [International Telecommunication Union](<https://www.itu.int/en/Pages/default.aspx>).

**Now:** Contributing to Cyber Resilience Act security standards at CEN-CENELEC JTC13/WG9,
steering the Product Security Working Group at the Connectivity Standards Alliance,
and serving as liaison between the CSA and CEN-CENELEC.

## Experience and expertise

My work sits at the intersection of technical implementation and standards-driven security:

-   Product security leadership and lifecycle management
-   Cybersecurity standards and certification (CRA, RED DA, EN IEC 62443, EN 303 645)
-   Operational Technology (OT) and IT security, including OT and IT integration
-   Network access control (IEEE 802.1X, 802.11i) and hardware-based security (secure elements, TPMs, TEEs)

I have applied this expertise across connected lighting and building automation (LiFi, NFC commissioning, IT network security),
automotive (V2V/V2I communication, telematics, OTA updates),
and mobile (TEE, secure element, NFC),
working with Zigbee, Matter, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and related connectivity technologies.


## Standards and certification

I help shape the future of connected product security by driving global standards and best practices,
and I bridge regulatory requirements and practical implementation.

A few contributions I am proud of:

-   [RED DA standards, EN 18031-1/2/3:2024](<https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/newsletter/ots-59-cybersecurity-standards/>):
    I played a key role in their development at CEN-CENELEC;
    they give manufacturers presumption of conformity under the Radio Equipment Directive
-   [Zigbee Direct](<https://csa-iot.org/all-solutions/zigbee/zigbee-direct/>), now part of the Zigbee Core specification
-   [LiFi and Power Line Communication ITU standards](<https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.9991/en>),
    including enterprise security based on IEEE 802.1X network access control
-   [Fairhair specification](<https://openconnectivity.org/developer/specifications/fairhair/>),
    the security architecture for IoT in commercial buildings

I am currently contributing to Cyber Resilience Act-related standards at CEN-CENELEC and ETSI.
On the compliance side I have worked with CRA, RED DA, IEC 62443, NIST SP 800-82, and UL 2900,
helping organizations achieve secure, certified, and future-ready products.
