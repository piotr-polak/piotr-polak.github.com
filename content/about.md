I help manufacturers turn cybersecurity requirements into secure connected products and practical compliance processes.

Over the past twenty years, I have worked at Philips, NXP, and now Signify. My experience spans connected-product development, security architecture, and certification. Alongside that work, I contribute to product security standards through CEN-CENELEC, ETSI, the Connectivity Standards Alliance (CSA), and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU).

My current focus is the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA): contributing to its supporting standards at CEN-CENELEC and ETSI, and connecting regulatory requirements with engineering practice.

## How I can help

- Interpret how the CRA, the Radio Equipment Directive Delegated Act (RED DA), and related standards apply to your products
- Define a practical compliance path, from cybersecurity risk assessment to conformity assessment
- Strengthen product security architecture, lifecycle processes, and vulnerability handling
- Prepare for CRA reporting obligations
- Speak at your event or host a webinar on connected-product security and compliance

For speaking engagements, standardization cooperation, or advisory questions, [email me](mailto:contact@piotrpolak.com) or connect on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/piotrpolak).

## Experience and expertise

My work connects three areas:

### Product security architecture and lifecycle management

Security risk assessment, vulnerability handling, hardware-based security, and network access control—including secure elements, trusted platform modules, trusted execution environments, and IEEE 802.1X.

### Regulation, standards, and conformity assessment

Experience with the CRA and RED DA, alongside standards and guidance including IEC 62443, EN 303 645, NIST SP 800-82, and UL 2900.

### Connected-product engineering

Experience across connected lighting and building automation, automotive systems, and mobile devices. This includes LiFi, NFC commissioning, vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure communication, telematics, and over-the-air updates, using technologies such as Zigbee, Matter, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.

## Contributions to standards

- [EN 18031-1/2/3:2024](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/newsletter/ots-59-cybersecurity-standards/) — I played a key role in developing these cybersecurity standards at CEN-CENELEC, supporting manufacturers’ conformity assessment under the RED DA.
- [Zigbee Direct](https://csa-iot.org/all-solutions/zigbee/zigbee-direct/) — I contributed to this specification, now part of the Zigbee Core specification.
- [ITU LiFi and Power Line Communication standards](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.9991/en) — I contributed to these standards, including enterprise security based on IEEE 802.1X network access control.
- [Fairhair specification](https://openconnectivity.org/developer/specifications/fairhair/) — I contributed to this security architecture for IoT in commercial buildings.

## Standardization roles

### CEN-CENELEC

At CEN-CENELEC, I represent the Dutch national committee through the [Cybersecurity & Privacy standards committee](https://www.nen.nl/en/normcommissie-cybersecurity-privacy) and [NEC 65](https://www.nen.nl/normcommissie-industrieel-meten-regelen-en-automatiseren-nec-65).

- **JTC13:** Working on RED DA standards (WG 8) and CRA horizontal standards (WG 9).
- **TC65X:** Working on CRA standards based on IEC 62443 (WG 3).
- **TC205:** Focused on IT security for home and building systems (WG 20).

### ETSI

I act as liaison between CEN-CENELEC JTC13 WG 9 and ETSI. At ETSI, I contribute mainly to the CRA standard for smart home products with security functionalities, such as door locks, cameras, and alarms ([EN 304 632](https://portal.etsi.org/webapp/WorkProgram/Report_WorkItem.asp?WKI_ID=74417)).

### Connectivity Standards Alliance

I serve on the Security Advisory Group and the Product Security Working Group Steering Committee. I also act as liaison between the CSA and CEN-CENELEC.

### International Telecommunication Union

My contributions include work on G.9991.
