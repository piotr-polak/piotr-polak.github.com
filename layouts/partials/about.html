I help manufacturers of connected products make sense of EU cybersecurity regulation: the Cyber Resilience Act, the RED Delegated Act, and the security standards behind them.

I drive the development and certification of secure connected products and contribute to global product security standards through my work with CEN-CENELEC, the Connectivity Standards Alliance, and the International Telecommunication Union.
