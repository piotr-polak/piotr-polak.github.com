I help manufacturers of connected products make sense of EU cybersecurity regulation: the Cyber Resilience Act, the RED Delegated Act, and the standards that support compliance.

My work combines connected-product development, security architecture, and certification with contributions to product security standards at CEN-CENELEC, the Connectivity Standards Alliance, and the International Telecommunication Union.
